WHO WE ARE

Flowers Made with Love

Welcome to Fleur & Fuzz, a handmade bouquet business created to share happiness through flowers.

We create fuzzy wire bouquets with care and creativity. Each piece is designed to be a special gift for birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, and other memorable occasions.

Our goal is to turn simple moments into beautiful memories with flowers that can be treasured for a long time.
